Timetable Tamer

Support

Timetable Tamer gives teachers a glanceable countdown and a discreet wrist tap before every lesson bell, on iPhone and Apple Watch. Set your timetable once and it runs every school day.

Getting started

  • Days — build your day types (e.g. “Normal Day”, “Late Start”) with the bell times for each period.
  • Week — assign a day type to each weekday (with weekly or fortnightly Week A / Week B support).
  • Today — see the live countdown to the next bell; swipe between days to check ahead.
  • Settings — choose warning times, which bells buzz, which device taps you, and calibrate to your school's real bell.

Common questions

  • Not feeling the wrist tap? Make sure notifications are allowed for Timetable Tamer in iOS/watchOS Settings, and that your chosen device is set under Settings ▸ buzz on.
  • Bell a little early or late? Use the calibration setting to shift every time by a few seconds to match your school's bell.
  • Moving to a new phone? Use Save a backup to export your timetable, then restore it on the new device.
