Timetable Tamer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Timetable Tamer is designed to be private. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Timetable Tamer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

What the app stores

  • Your timetable and settings (day types, bell times, class names, room numbers, holidays, warning preferences, and calibration) are stored locally on your device. On Apple Watch, the same schedule is shared with the watch app and its complication via Apple's on-device app group and synced from your iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity — it does not leave your devices.
  • Notifications used to tap your wrist before a bell are scheduled locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch. They are not sent through any server.

Backups (optional, you control them)

If you choose to use the in-app “Save a backup” feature, the app writes a file containing your schedule to the location you pick (for example, iCloud Drive via the Files app). That file is stored in your own iCloud/Files account under your control; the developer has no access to it. Restoring reads a file you select. The app never uploads your data anywhere automatically.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect analytics or usage data.
  • It does not include advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not use third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It does not require an account or any sign-in.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.

Notifications

The app requests permission to send notifications solely to deliver your bell warnings and wrist taps. You can change this at any time in iOS/watchOS Settings.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “last updated” date.
